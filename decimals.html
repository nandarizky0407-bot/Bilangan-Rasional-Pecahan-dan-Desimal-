✖️ Multiplying Decimals — Two Methods

Method 1: Fraction Method

Example: 0.3 × 0.4 = ?

Step 1: Convert to fractions
  0.3 = 3/10
  0.4 = 4/10

Step 2: Multiply numerators and denominators directly
  (do NOT simplify yet!)
  3/10 × 4/10 = (3×4)/(10×10) = 12/100

Step 3: Convert back to decimal
  12/100 = 0.12

So, 0.3 × 0.4 = 0.12 💖

Method 2: Direct Method

Example: 0.3 × 0.4 = ?

Step 1: Multiply the numbers (ignore decimal points)
  3 × 4 = 12

Step 2: Count total decimal places
  0.3 has 1 decimal place
  0.4 has 1 decimal place
  Total: 1 + 1 = 2 decimal places

Step 3: Place the decimal point 2 places from the right
  12 → 0.12

So, 0.3 × 0.4 = 0.12 ✨

➗ Dividing Decimals — Two Methods

Method 1: Fraction Method

Example: 0.8 ÷ 0.2 = ?

Step 1: Convert to fractions
  0.8 = 8/10
  0.2 = 2/10

Step 2: Divide fractions (multiply by reciprocal)
  8/10 ÷ 2/10 = 8/10 × 10/2
  = (8×10)/(10×2) = 80/20

Step 3: Simplify
  80/20 = 4

So, 0.8 ÷ 0.2 = 4 💫

Method 2: Direct Division

Example: 0.8 ÷ 0.2 = ?

Step 1: Make the divisor a whole number
  Multiply both by 10:
  0.8 × 10 = 8
  0.2 × 10 = 2

Step 2: Divide normally
  8 ÷ 2 = 4

So, 0.8 ÷ 0.2 = 4 🌟

What We Learned Today

✖️
Decimals can be multiplied by converting to fractions first, then multiplying numerators directly (without simplifying) before converting back.
🔢
Direct multiplication: multiply the numbers ignoring decimals, then count total decimal places to place the point correctly.
➗
Decimals can be divided by converting to fractions and multiplying by the reciprocal.
💪
Direct division: make the divisor a whole number by multiplying both numbers, then divide normally.
